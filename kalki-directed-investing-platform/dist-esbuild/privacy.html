Privacy Policy

Kalki Analysis provides a self-directed alert review and broker connection platform. This policy explains what information may be collected when customers connect sources, receive WhatsApp notifications, review alerts, and connect their own broker accounts.

Information We Collect

We may collect account profile details such as name, email address, WhatsApp phone number, connected source preferences, broker type, broker account identifiers, risk settings, approval settings, alert review records, order intent records, order status records, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the platform.

Broker API credentials, when provided, are sent to the backend for broker connection and are intended to be stored encrypted. We do not intentionally display broker secrets back to the user after saving.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide the service, send WhatsApp approval notifications, connect to customer-selected broker accounts, present alert and order review workflows, maintain audit records, troubleshoot issues, improve platform reliability, and comply with legal, security, and platform requirements.

WhatsApp and Meta

If a customer opts in to WhatsApp notifications, the customer's WhatsApp number and message content may be sent to Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API to deliver the notification.

Financial Disclaimer

Kalki Analysis is not a financial adviser, investment adviser, broker-dealer, fiduciary, tax adviser, or legal adviser. Alerts and notifications are educational information and platform workflow data only. Customers remain responsible for reviewing risks, deciding whether to place any order, and managing their own brokerage accounts.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers used to operate the platform, including hosting providers, communications providers, broker APIs selected by the customer, and compliance or security providers when needed. We do not sell customer broker credentials.

Retention and Deletion

We retain information as needed to operate the platform, maintain audit records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect the service. Customers may request deletion using our Data Deletion Instructions.
