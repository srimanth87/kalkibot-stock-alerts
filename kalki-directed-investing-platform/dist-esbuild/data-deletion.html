Data Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of personal information associated with Kalki Analysis and the Kalki Directed Investing platform.

How to Request Deletion

Email kalkibot87@gmail.com with the subject line "Kalki Data Deletion Request".

Please include enough information to identify the account, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your WhatsApp phone number, if used for notifications
  • The broker account label or connected source name, if applicable

What We Delete

After verifying the request, we will delete or de-identify personal profile information, WhatsApp notification details, connected source settings, broker connection records, and other platform records where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Records We May Retain

Some records may be retained when needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, financial audit trails, or broker/order activity records. Retained records will be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

Meta App Removal

If you connected through a Meta or WhatsApp-related workflow, you may also remove the app from your Meta account settings where applicable.
